# Reader demonstration evidence

The original brief’s component markup, captured CSS and embedded Inter fonts are reused in brief-demo.html. All assessment data and persona biographies are illustrative. The provided live brief remains a design reference, not demonstration content.

- Andrade (2019), A Critical Review of Research on Student Self-Assessment. Review of 76 studies; strongest rationale is formative use with training and opportunities for adjustment, not a claim that selecting a grade guarantees learning. https://doi.org/10.3389/feduc.2019.00087
- Nicol & Macfarlane-Dick (2006), formative assessment and self-regulated learning. Supports explicit expectations and learner judgement. https://doi.org/10.1080/03075070600572090
- W3C COGA clear page structure supports headings, grouping and orientation. https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG2/supplemental/patterns/o2p03-page-structure/
- CAST UDL organising information and monitoring progress inform user-controlled detail and checking. https://udlguidelines.cast.org/action-expression/strategy-development/organize-information/ and https://udlguidelines.cast.org/action-expression/strategy-development/monitor-progress/

The personas supplied in the reference are worked examples. We have no underlying evidence rows, so the demonstration personas are explicitly fictional scenarios and carry no invented research IDs. Disciplinary briefs demonstrate configuration rather than measured differences between disciplines.
