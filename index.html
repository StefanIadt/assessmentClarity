05 / THE PROJECTRESEARCH IN PROGRESS

Universal Design
for Assessment.

This interactive experience accompanies Universal Design for Assessment: A Connected Approach to Student Success, a SATLE-funded initiative at the Institute of Art, Design and Technology (IADT).

The working prototype connects assessment creation, student-facing briefs and planning, programme-level scheduling, submission, feedback and grading. It also supports transparent guidance on generative AI use.

Development has been informed by staff consultation and student use across multiple programmes. Evaluation is ongoing. The interactive examples on this site are illustrative, not evidence of measured student outcomes.
