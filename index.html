Assessment design should reduce unnecessary cognitive load, not add to it.

What does that mean for an assessment brief?

How information is grouped, connected and revealed matters.

Structure.

The assessment brief uses consistent headings and a clear order, so students know where to find the task, submission requirements and assessment criteria.

Design rationale: W3C ↗

Scroll to assemble the parts.

  1. Structure: group related information within a predictable hierarchy.
  2. Manageable detail: open a group to explore its detail without losing context.
  3. Alignment: connect intended learning, opportunities to demonstrate it, and assessment criteria.
  4. Planning: make stages and milestones visible.
  5. Checking and reflection: revisit requirements and assess progress.
