Requirements · excerpt from slide 6

Keep the task in view.

The task stays above its supporting detail. Try opening the groups below.

What should the website help users do?

Based on initial research, the primary goals of the users are as follows:

  • Be aware of all relevant opportunities as they arise.
  • Have sufficient information to make informed choices.
  • Reduce the time spent filtering non-relevant opportunities.
  • Save and recall specific information for later review.
  • Share chosen information with others.
What should the work consider?

The primary focus of this assignment is to create an inclusive design that addresses the needs of users with disabilities. Additionally, you will need to consider the information architecture, content design, and visual design of the website.

Where can I find supporting resources?

The presentation groups resources into academic sources, websites and podcasts. The completed brief will supply the actual links and any required reading.

Behaviour

Native expandable sections work by pointer, touch and keyboard. Opening one does not close another. Scrolling never changes the reader’s choices.

Needed from the completed brief

Complete guidance and resource links. Preserve essential requirements even when detail is collapsed.

Source: Presentation pp. 6, 11. CAST UDL: organise information and resources.
